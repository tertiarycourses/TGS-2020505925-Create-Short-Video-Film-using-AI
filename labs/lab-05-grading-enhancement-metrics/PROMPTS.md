Grading and upscaling instructions for image tools and colour pages.

> **Currency note.** Tools and model names are examples of a technique, not endorsements, and AI video features change month to month. The prompt patterns here are tool-neutral. Before relying on a feature, clip length, resolution or price, check the provider's current documentation and terms, and record the model ID and date of every generation you keep.

## 1. Grade description (for a colour-grading preset or a still edit)

```text
Night-for-night blue hour: cool teal shadows, warm sodium and lantern practicals, lifted blacks kept below 5%, gentle film grain, skin and the yellow raincoat untouched in hue.
```

## 2. Upscale only the keepers

Generate every exploratory still and clip at the lowest acceptable resolution. Upscale only approved takes, then re-check SSIM against the draft for plastic skin and halos.
