# Lab 07 — Prompt and Specification Pack

**Look Bible and Scene Continuity with Global Descriptors**  
Create Short Video Film using AI (TGS-2020505925) · Version v12.0 · 27 September 2026

Look and lighting language that keeps every generated shot on the look bible.

> **Currency note.** Tools and model names are examples of a technique, not endorsements, and AI video features change month to month. The prompt patterns here are tool-neutral. Before relying on a feature, clip length, resolution or price, check the provider's current documentation and terms, and record the model ID and date of every generation you keep.

---

## 1. Look block (paste into every shot prompt)

```text
LOOK: blue-hour night, teal ambient shadows, warm sodium and lantern practicals, wet reflective ground, light rain, restrained saturation except neon signs, 35mm film grain. Never daylight.
```

## 2. Carry the look between scenes

Stage the characters inside the approved location plate as a still first, grade that still to the look bible, and animate the graded still. The model then inherits the look instead of inventing it.
