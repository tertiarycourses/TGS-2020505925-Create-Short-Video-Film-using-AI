# Lab 09 — Prompt and Specification Pack

**Storyboard to Shot Prompts, Start and End Frames, and the Animatic**  
Create Short Video Film using AI (TGS-2020505925) · Version v12.0 · 27 September 2026

Shot prompts in three formats. Pick the one your video tool follows best; keep the other two as the record.

> **Currency note.** Tools and model names are examples of a technique, not endorsements, and AI video features change month to month. The prompt patterns here are tool-neutral. Before relying on a feature, clip length, resolution or price, check the provider's current documentation and terms, and record the model ID and date of every generation you keep.

---

## 1. Multi-shot prompt

```text
Shot 1: [action], [camera].
Shot 2: [action], [camera]. MEI (breathless): "Last ferry leaves at midnight."
Shot 3: [action], [camera].
```
One shot per line, in story order; dialogue in quotation marks is read as a voice line.

## 2. Timeline prompt

```text
[0-2s] wide, slow pan right: lanterns sway, the camera finds MEI.
[2-5s] she weaves between stalls clutching the red box.
[5-8s] she checks her watch without slowing.
```

## 3. Six-point video prompt

SUBJECT · ACTION · ENVIRONMENT · CAMERA · STYLE · CONSTRAINTS — the same fields as Lab 03, plus duration, aspect ratio, number of takes and the start/end frame files.

## 4. Continuity controls

Start frame = where the shot begins. End frame = where it must land. Video reference / extension = pass the whole previous clip so lighting, mood and motion carry over. Generate three takes only for dense action.
