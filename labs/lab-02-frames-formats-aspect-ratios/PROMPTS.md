# Lab 02 — Prompt and Specification Pack

**Frames, Formats and Aspect Ratios for Film Delivery**  
Create Short Video Film using AI (TGS-2020505925) · Version v12.0 · 27 September 2026

Format and delivery specifications to paste into a generation tool or a brief.

> **Currency note.** Tools and model names are examples of a technique, not endorsements, and AI video features change month to month. The prompt patterns here are tool-neutral. Before relying on a feature, clip length, resolution or price, check the provider's current documentation and terms, and record the model ID and date of every generation you keep.

---

## 1. Format block for every shot prompt

```text
Aspect ratio 16:9, 24 fps, 1920x1080 master. Keep the subject, faces, the red box and any on-screen text inside the centre third of the frame so a 9:16 cut-down keeps them. No text in the top 14% or bottom 20%.
```

## 2. Native vertical variant

```text
Aspect ratio 9:16, 1080x1920, vertical composition: subject framed from the waist up, headroom 8%, caption space kept clear in the lower fifth.
```
