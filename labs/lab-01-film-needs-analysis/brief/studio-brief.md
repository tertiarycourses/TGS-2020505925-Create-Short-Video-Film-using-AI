# Client brief — Harbourline Ferries (fictional)

**From:** Head of Brand, Harbourline Ferries
**To:** Straits Harbour Studio (your team)
**Re:** Which of our six film ideas should use an AI production workflow?

Harbourline Ferries turns 50 next quarter. Six departments have each asked the studio
for "AI video". We have one production budget and one small team, so we need a
defensible shortlist, not six half-finished films.

## Hard constraints

1. **Likeness and consent.** No real passenger or staff face or voice may be generated,
   cloned or altered without written consent. This is a PDPA and brand-trust rule,
   not a preference.
2. **Rights.** Anything that depicts regulated safety equipment needs the maritime
   regulator's sign-off before release.
3. **Authenticity.** Factual statements by real executives are filmed, not generated.
4. **Budget.** The AI production line must cost at least 30% less per quarter than the
   current approach for the project to be worth re-tooling.

## What we need from you

- A scored shortlist with a one-sentence rationale for every project you reject or hold.
- For each selected project, the workflow pattern you recommend (all-in-one agent,
  multi-tool pipeline, or hybrid/local) and why.
- For the anniversary film **The Last Ferry**, a five-stage production pipeline
  specification we can hand to the crew.

*All companies, people and figures in this brief are fictional classroom data.*
