# Lab 11 — Prompt and Specification Pack

**Voiceover, Music, Captions and the Final Export Ladder**  
Create Short Video Film using AI (TGS-2020505925) · Version v12.0 · 27 September 2026

Voice, music and sound-effect prompts, written to the cue sheet.

> **Currency note.** Tools and model names are examples of a technique, not endorsements, and AI video features change month to month. The prompt patterns here are tool-neutral. Before relying on a feature, clip length, resolution or price, check the provider's current documentation and terms, and record the model ID and date of every generation you keep.

---

## 1. Voiceover with audio tags

```text
[out of breath] Don't drop it. Don't drop it.
[curious] No sender. Just a tag, folded shut.
```
Generate the voiceover first, measure each line, then cut the pictures to it. Keep the same voice ID for a character in every clip so the voice stays continuous.

## 2. Music

```text
Instrumental, sparse felt piano over a soft synth pulse, melancholy building to a warm resolve, 86 BPM, no vocals, ends on a sustained major chord.
```

## 3. Sound effects

```text
Steady light rain on a timber pier, distant harbour ambience; one deep ferry horn, 1.5 seconds, slight echo.
```
